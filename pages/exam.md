<h1 align="center">Exam</h1>

### Exam prerequisites

None.

### Exam type

The exam is a **20-minute oral examination**.

It starts from **one of the six assignments** that the group completed during the semester. The examiner also discusses the **group project**.

The final grade is an overall assessment of the assignment work and the oral examination. Internal assessment. Grading uses the Danish 7-point scale.

You should be able to:

- explain the engineering question behind the chosen assignment;
- justify the statistical method, including assumptions;
- interpret numerical output in context, not just read a \(p\)-value;
- discuss limitations, uncertainty, and what you would do with more data;
- relate the assignment to the broader project analysis.

### Tools allowed

None during the oral examination itself. You may bring printed copies of your assignment and project if the examiner asks you to walk through a figure or a table.

### Re-exam

Same as the ordinary exam.

### How the course prepares you

The six assignments and the project are both learning activities and exam preparation. Session 12 collects a method-choice guide and a full worked analysis in the same style as the project.
